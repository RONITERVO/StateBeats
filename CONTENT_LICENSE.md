# Original content

The contributors dedicate the original StateBeats map data and procedural music
to CC0 1.0 Universal, **except the Ink-Battle collaboration below**. Code implementing
the StateBeats compiler and its original audio synthesis is MIT.

Full legal text is in `LICENSE-CC0.txt`. Third-party dependencies retain their own
licenses, collected in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`. No commercial songs or sample packs
are bundled.

The **Ink-Battle — Between the Lines** map, its generated JSON export in
`packages/content/maps/ink-battle-between-the-lines.json`, original soundtrack,
battle recordings, and everything in `packages/ink-battle` are **Apache-2.0**.
That package contains original Ink-Battle procedural models, paper textures and
simulation code; it is not CC0. See its `LICENSE`, `NOTICE` and `PROVENANCE.json`
for the pinned upstream revision and file hashes. `scripts/record-ink-battle.mjs`
and `scripts/compose-ink-battle.py` are also Apache-2.0.

Distributing the collaboration requires retaining its Apache license and notices.
The static player build and release packages include them. The `@statebeats/content`
catalog references this separately licensed package; its original maps remain CC0.
