An engine for any player.

StateBeats is a spatial rhythm simulation with explicit time. A person, bot or language-model agent submits the same pose commands. Graphics and sound describe the result; the engine decides it.

Play

Desktop: aim with the mouse, left button for the left hand, right for the right. Each hand stays where you pressed while held. Space places both hands at your aim. Q/E turn; Escape pauses. C ducks beneath passing birds.

Quest: open the HTTPS build in Quest Browser, enter immersive VR, point and pull the trigger to choose a sequence. Grip opens the menu. Touch notes with the indicated hands, hold sustained notes and avoid red volumes. The standalone hardware playtest is pending.

Hear the space

Cyan/left cues are lower; coral/right cues are higher. Together makes a chord. Holds sound longer, hazards sound low and rough. Height shifts pitch. A rear note adds a second knock. Timing rings and repeated sound cues lead into the beat.

Choose your perception

Settings independently control music, cues, captions, contrast and reduced motion, and remember your choices on this device. Hand glyphs, timing rings and captions also support muted play. The text player uses explicit steps and keyboard controls, without graphics or audio. Its assisted timing sends ordinary engine commands.

Follow the sun

Chasing the sun has a traveling sun and trail, approaching stars, paired touches, stationary holds and passing birds across a full turn. A sky full of stars presents that journey in space. Both share the same gameplay rules and replay format.

Open Your music & community maps to select a local song, enter its BPM and generate a chart. Export the JSON to keep its notes, scene paths and music features. Audio remains on your device; choose the matching file again when loading a saved chart. Imported files are limited to 40 MB and ten minutes; supported formats depend on browser decoding.

Build on it

The source includes a dependency-free core, SDK, map builder, versioned replays, CLI and real MCP server. Actor, perception, clock, storage, interaction, scoring and arbitration adapters are replaceable. 

npm ci
npm run build
npm run demo
node examples/headless.mjs
