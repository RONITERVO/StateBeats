A FEW NOTES BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Reach on the beat.

In VRTouch the cyan notes with your left hand, coral with your right, and white with either. Gold needs both hands; rings need a sustained touch. Avoid red volumes with hands and head.

Turn with the sequenceLight trails and directional sound announce where the next note is. The small compass cue points toward notes behind you. Use grip/squeeze to open the menu in VR, then point and trigger to choose.

On desktopMove the mouse to aim. Left click reaches with your left hand, right click with your right. Drag the last pressed hand; the other retains its aim. The pointer assists depth at nearby targets. Q/E turns, up/down arrows look vertically. Both clicks together touch gold notes. Hold C to duck beneath birds. Escape pauses.

Audio cuesCyan: low glass tone. Coral: high bell. Gold: a chord. A rear target has a double knock, a front target one; pitch also rises with height. A repeating pulse counts down to the hit. Red buzz means avoidance.

YOUR PRACTICE SPACE

Make it yours.

Applies on your next start or restart, to every map. 1.20× asks for wider reaches. Height adjusts the vertical layout; note sizes stay the same.

Timing offsets adjust sound presentation. They never change authoritative scores.

Guidance tells you where and when to act. World effects follow moving targets and soften while guidance sounds play. All three volumes are saved on this device.
